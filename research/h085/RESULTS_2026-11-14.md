Exploratory cross-variant stationary-block bootstrap max-mean statistic: approximately **p=0.9983** over 28 candidates; the best candidate's average monthly excess over SPY was negative. This cannot establish a statistically significant edge, even before accounting for reused sample selection.

## Interpretation and next phase

1. The premise that retrospective winning sub-strategies can be combined into a reliably superior next-period portfolio **failed this test**.
2. H085 is a **diagnostic negative result**, not a proposal to optimize the same 45 months until success. Do not revise filter thresholds in response to these outcomes.
3. The next useful direction is a new **prospectively frozen, independently sourced, execution-costed** hypothesis with a truly unseen holdout, rather than applying more selectors to already mined H083 data.
4. Runtime operations remain separate: successful H085 GitHub Actions is not evidence that the actual main-branch Arena or Paper Watch scheduler ran successfully after PR #11.
5. Source provenance and logs remain available via the H085 Actions artifact and `H085_REPORT.json`. The source frozen rotation/event ledger checksums were verified against H084's independent hashes.
